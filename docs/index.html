Veilor documentation

Veilor is a read-only console for prediction markets, with a wallet view for Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663). It lists live markets from Polymarket, shows their real order books and rules, estimates a model fair line for crypto and gold price questions, and shows the cost of an order before you trade. It never holds your funds and never places or signs orders. This page reads without JavaScript. The full interactive guide is at veilor.markets/#/docs.

Where the data comes from

Markets, prices, volume, rules, order books, price history and trades: Polymarket public APIs. Spot prices for BTC, ETH, SOL and gold: Binance. Balances, tokens and transactions on Robinhood Chain: its public RPC and the Blockscout explorer. Any of them can be delayed or wrong, so check the venue before you trade. The fair line is a model estimate and can be wrong.

Console tools

  • Markets, watchlist, new listings, edges. Live markets with order books and venue rules.
  • Order ticket. Walks the live order book and shows the average fill, price impact and cost. It sends no order.
  • Payoff calculator (#/calc). Turns a price and an amount into shares, payout, profit, loss and break-even probability. A link can reopen it with your numbers.
  • Trade journal (#/journal). A private log stored in your browser, with statistics, CSV export and CSV import.
  • Alerts and the Telegram bot @VeilorAlertsBot.
  • Price is probability (#/learn). An explainer and a five-question quiz.

Developers

Read-only endpoints and an MCP server. Nothing to sign, no keys, no funds. Free-tier limits apply and uptime is not guaranteed.

MCP server: https://veilor-worker.yansahsick.workers.dev/mcp
Tools: list_markets, get_market, get_order_book, get_prices, fair_line.

HTTP endpoints:
GET https://veilor-worker.yansahsick.workers.dev/api/prices
GET https://veilor-worker.yansahsick.workers.dev/api/markets
GET https://veilor-worker.yansahsick.workers.dev/cal/all.ics (calendar feed; per category: /cal/<category>.ics)

Example: curl https://veilor-worker.yansahsick.workers.dev/api/prices

The endpoints have no written limits or versioning yet. They are listed here as they are today.

Security and trust

No audit is claimed. To report a security issue, send a direct message to @VeilorMarkets on X first and do not post it in public. The contact is also in security.txt. What Veilor never does, and what it stores, is on the Trust page.
